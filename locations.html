Find us

On shelves and pouring across Sri Lanka.

Epitome is in select supermarkets, and a growing circle of cafés is pouring it across the island. Here is where to find it.

Retail

In supermarkets, now.

Since last month, Epitome has been on shelves in select supermarkets across Sri Lanka. The list is growing — ask for Epitome where you shop, or reserve a bag directly from the house.

Small coffee shops across the island are joining us — selling and pouring Epitome. The first locations are being added now; check back soon, or visit the supermarkets above in the meantime.
